Income Amplifier Terms

Loan Constant

The annual debt service (principal + interest) expressed as a percentage of the original loan amount. Used to quickly compare borrowing costs across different loan structures. In the Income Amplifier, the 75% loan constant determines the cash flow rate for each cycle.

Source: George Antone -- Income Amplifier Blueprint, Ch. 6 (Smart Levers) [Video] @ Income Amplifier Course > Criteria Module

Cash Flow Rate

The net income generated per amplifier flip cycle after deducting borrowing costs. Must be positive for the amplifier to work.

Source: Fynanc Academy -- Income Amplifier Course > Criteria Module [Video] @ Income Amplifier Course > Criteria Module

Income Amplifier

A capital-cycling strategy that uses a credit facility (HELOC/LOC) as a funding line. Borrow at one rate, invest at a higher rate, earn the spread, repay, and repeat.

Source: George Antone -- The Income Amplifier Blueprint, Ch. 7 (Step-by-Step Blueprint) [Video] @ Income Amplifier Blueprint > Ch. 7

Spread

The difference between the yield earned on an investment and the cost of borrowing to fund it. Spread = Investment Yield - LOC Borrowing Rate.

Source: Fynanc Academy -- Income Amplifier Course > Selection Criteria [Video] @ Income Amplifier Course > Criteria Module

Grow Capital Phase 🔄

Year 1-2 of the Fynanc wealth journey. The accumulation phase where the Income Amplifier builds capital. Focus all Thrust on growing capital size before moving to Invest phase. Scattered effort across phases delays wealth building by decades.

Grow Capital definition: Year 1-2 of the Fynanc wealth journey. The accumulation phase where the Income Amplifier builds capital for later PLEX deployment.
Source: George Antone -- Fynanc Substack: Why the Order of Operations Changes Everything (May 2026) [Video] (See full session)

Forgiveness Flip

A specific Income Amplifier strategy type that incorporates a forgiveness component in the credit structure.

Source: Fynanc Academy -- Income Amplifier Course > Strategy Types [Video] @ Income Amplifier Course > Strategy Types

Copycat Booster

A proven Income Amplifier flip pattern shared within the Fynanc community. Members replicate established playbooks.

Source: Fynanc Academy -- Income Amplifier Course > Copycat Booster Strategy [Video] @ Income Amplifier Course > Community Strategies

IRIS Plan

The Fynanc financial planning tool that calculates optimal LOC sizes, contribution rates, and timelines for achieving passive income targets.

Source: Fynanc Academy -- IRIS Academy > IRIS Plan Review Module [Video] @ IRIS Academy > Plan Review Module

FLOW

Fynanc's automated cash flow management system covering 4 stages: awareness, optimization, systemization, and mastery of money movement. Automates transfers between Income Hub, Sweep Account, and Bill Pay to maximize capital velocity.

Source: George Antone -- FLOW Training Course > Introduction Module [Video] @ FLOW Training > Introduction > Lesson 05

Family Banking

A strategy to redirect income streams that normally flow to commercial banks back to yourself using a private banking structure. Based on the concept that your income is a source of income streams to banks, and you can capture that value instead.

Source: Fynanc Academy -- Family Bank / Personal Banking Course [Video] @ Family Bank Course

Velocity of Money

The concept of cycling capital through multiple uses quickly rather than letting it sit idle. Each dollar does multiple jobs in a short time frame, compounding returns without needing additional capital.

Source: Fynanc Academy -- Income Sources > Senior Loans (How to Use with IA) [Video] @ Income Sources > Senior Loans Module

Capital Amplifier

The overarching wealth operating system encompassing IRIS, Income Amplifier, and FLOW. It is the machine that cycles capital through the R-C Loop (Return-Capital Loop) repeatedly to accelerate wealth building.

Source: George Antone -- FLOW Training > Worldview Module (6 Components) [Video] @ FLOW Training > Introduction > Worldview (Lesson 08)

Money Date

A scheduled recurring review session where you implement action plans from each Fynanc strategy. Your Money Date Playbook contains all action plans organized for systematic execution.

Source: Fynanc Academy -- FLOW Training > Money Date Action Plan [Video] @ FLOW Training > Introduction > Lesson 04

Income Stream

A regular series of payments from one party to another. Wealth building requires moving from the paying side to the receiving side of income streams. Banks profit from income streams generated by your labor.

Source: George Antone -- FLOW Training > Tier 1 Awareness [Video] @ FLOW Training > Tier 1 Awareness (Lesson 02)

PLEX Terms

PLEX Portfolio

A margin-based investment portfolio structured into three allocation tiers (Bedrock 65%, Cash Flow 34%, Hedge 1%).

Source: George Antone -- PLEX Immersion Course, Call 1 (Jan 26, 2026) [Video] @ PLEX Course > Overview (Call 1)

Bedrock (Tier)

The foundation tier of PLEX, comprising minimum 65% of portfolio value. Selected for price stability, low max drawdown, low market correlation.

Source: George Antone -- PLEX Course Call 1, Workbook p.66 (Jan 26, 2026) [Video] @ PLEX Course > Call 1, Page 66 Workbook

Cash Flow (Tier)

The income-generating tier of PLEX, comprising 34% of portfolio value. High yield, accepts NAV erosion and higher volatility.

Source: George Antone -- PLEX Course Call 1 (Jan 26, 2026) [Video] @ PLEX Course > Call 1

Hedge (Tier)

The protection tier of PLEX, 1% of portfolio. Moves opposite direction. Asymmetric payoff potential.

Source: George Antone -- PLEX Course Call 1 (Jan 26, 2026) [Video] @ PLEX Course > Call 1

Price Stability

The #1 priority criterion for Bedrock tier selection. Low standard deviation of price over time.

Source: George Antone -- PLEX Course Call 1, Workbook p.66 (Jan 26, 2026) [Video] @ PLEX Course > Call 1, Workbook p.66

Max Drawdown

Maximum peak-to-trough decline in a ticker's price. Check 5-10 year chart for crash survival.

Source: George Antone -- PLEX Course Call 2 (Feb 2, 2026) [Video] @ PLEX Course > Call 2 (Feb 2, 2026)

Market Correlation

How closely a ticker's price movements track the overall stock market. Seek non-correlated for Bedrock.

Source: George Antone -- PLEX Course Call 1 (Jan 26, 2026) [Video] @ PLEX Course > Call 1

Maintenance Requirement (IMR)

Minimum equity a brokerage requires when using margin. Lower IMR = more margin available. M1: 45%, Schwab: 100%, IB: 25%.

Source: George Antone -- PLEX Course Call 3 > Margin Health Analyzer (Feb 9, 2026) [Video] @ PLEX Course > Call 3 (Feb 9, 2026)

Sub-Pie Allocation

The percentage weight of an individual ticker within its tier. Each tier sums to 100% internally.

Source: Fynanc Academy -- PLEX M1 Sub-Pie Setup Session (Feb 2, 2026) [Video] @ PLEX Course > M1 Sub-Pie Setup (Feb 2, 2026)

DSCR

Debt Service Coverage Ratio. Safety metric measuring whether portfolio income covers debt obligations. Non-negotiable.

Source: George Antone -- PLEX Course > Safety & Guardrails Module [Video] @ PLEX Course > Safety Module

DTA

Debt-to-Asset Ratio. Must remain below 40%. Exceeding puts portfolio in margin call danger territory.

Source: George Antone -- PLEX Course > Safety & Guardrails Module [Video] @ PLEX Course > Safety Module

NAV Erosion

Gradual decline in Net Asset Value in high-yield funds legally required to distribute earnings. Accepted in Cash Flow tier.

Source: George Antone -- PLEX Course Call 1 (Jan 26, 2026) [Video] @ PLEX Course > Call 1

Super Vault

A capital structure component in the Fynanc Toolbox for secure capital parking and leverage management within the PLEX system.

Source: Fynanc Academy -- PLEX Tools > Capital Structure Module [Video] @ PLEX Tools > Smart Credit Line Module

Yield Stacking

An advanced PLEX strategy that layers multiple yield sources on the same capital base -- dividends, margin interest arbitrage, appreciation -- to maximize total return per dollar deployed.

Source: Fynanc Academy -- PLEX Course > Advanced Yield Strategies [Video] @ PLEX Course > Advanced Strategies

Order of Operations

The sequential framework for building wealth: Grow Capital (increase pile size) -> Invest Capital (move capital into passive-flow structures) -> Preserve Capital (protect from taxes and inflation). Mixing phases slows progress; focusing all thrust on one phase at a time creates unstoppable momentum.

Source: George Antone -- Fynanc Substack (May 8 2026) [Video] (See full session)

Thrust

The concentrated financial effort applied to a single phase of the Order of Operations. Wealth Creators focus all their Thrust on one objective at a time rather than scattering effort across multiple goals.

Source: George Antone -- Fynanc Substack (May 8 2026) [Video] (See full session)

Preserve Capital Phase

The third phase of the Fynanc wealth journey. Protecting the wealth system from taxes and inflation. Should only be focused on AFTER Grow and Invest phases are established.

Source: George Antone -- Fynanc Substack (May 8 2026) [Video] (See full session)

Invest Capital Phase

The second phase of the Fynanc wealth journey. Moving grown capital into structures that produce passive cash flow (PLEX, FLOW, etc.).

Source: George Antone -- Fynanc Substack (May 8 2026) [Video] (See full session)
