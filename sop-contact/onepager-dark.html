TPJG FLEET — SOP Contact

The 7C Framework: Phase 1 — Contact
The 7 Contact SOPs
C01Lead Capture & Assignment
Trigger: New lead (Zillow, HAR, referral, OH, cold call)
  • VA-R sets up in CRM/RM; assign by territory
  • Contact within 5 min; categorize B/S/T/L
  • Begin nurture drip; DRS track for physicians
C02Open House Lead Capture
Trigger: Hosting an open house
  • Agent Packet + digital sign-in capture
  • Enter CRM within 24h; video text follow-up
  • Add to sphere drip campaign
C03Sphere of Influence Outreach
Trigger: Daily (non-negotiable)
  • 10 video texts/day (Krista Mashore method)
  • Personalize; DO NOT ask for business
  • Past clients, neighbors, businesses, occasions
C04Referral Generation & Tracking
Trigger: Any client interaction / networking
  • Big 3 Dialogue (Buffini); plant seeds naturally
  • Track source in RM; thank-you card in 48h
  • Log in Layer Map; update working agreements
C05Geographic Farming
Trigger: Quarterly marketing review
  • Target subdivision/zip; research stats
  • Monthly postcards + digital ads same geo
  • Track farming leads vs. other sources
C06Call Reluctance Mgmt
Trigger: Agent difficulty prospecting
  • ID fear pattern; NSOTD scripted openers
  • Role-play; warm calls first, then cold
  • Daily volume tracking; weekly coaching
C07Seller Pre-Qualification Script
Trigger: Inbound seller inquiry
  • “Never Split the Difference” master script — hear them first, qualify motivation/timeline/price, schedule consultation if qualified
  • Exception: Expired listing door-knock uses separate opener
Contact Taxonomy & Storage
Relationship Google Account Naming Convention CRM Group
Non-Business (friends, family) A@TPJG Standard name Sort B
Biz / Non-Realtor (vendors) M@TPJG Lastname INDUSTRY phone Sort B
Biz / Realtor (agents) M@TPJG Lastname CITY phone Agents + Sort B
Dual Storage: CRM/RM (source of truth) + contacts.google.com (A@TPJG or M@TPJG). Twins maintain sync.
UPSERT: Search lastname+phone/email → +firstname if multiple → UPDATE or INSERT → MERGE dupes
Daily Activities & Non-Negotiables
Activity Logging
  • All activities in CRM/RM
  • AI-logged = 🤖 emoji
  • Track: call, text, pop-by, note, email
  • Rotate types (never same twice in row)
Search Chain (Finding Contacts)
1. RM by lastname → 2. Firstname variants → 3. Gmail contacts → 4. BNI directory → 5. contacts.google.com
Daily Minimums
  • 10 video texts to sphere (C03)
  • 5-min speed-to-lead on new leads (C01)
  • Log every touch in CRM
  • Plant referral seeds every interaction (C04)
